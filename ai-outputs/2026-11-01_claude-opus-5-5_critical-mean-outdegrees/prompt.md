# Prompt and provenance

The manuscript was developed with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) in an interactive process of several
sessions in the Claude desktop app (Cowork), directed by Benedikt Jahnel. The model ran its own
simulations and exact computations and wrote all files in this folder.

The research direction was set by the author: study the critical mean out-degrees of the directed,
undirected and bidirectional lattice neighbour graphs with continuous parameter beta = 2dp, combine
simulations with rigorous bounds, and merge the earlier partial results (bidirectional graph for k=3,
d=2; directed graph via column projection; monotonicity in the dimension) into one manuscript.

Later instructions concerned editorial matters (file names, figures, funding, references, an
independent referee check by a separate agent and the resulting corrections).

The complete multi-session transcript is not included here.
